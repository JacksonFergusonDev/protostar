03 / MUTATION TESTING

Can the tests catch a bug?

Mutation testing introduces small deliberate bugs into selected engine modules. The score is the share caught by a failing test or timeout, out of caught, surviving, and suspicious mutants. Mutants no test reaches are excluded. This measures the modules listed below, not the whole codebase.

The overall line combines raw counts, so larger modules carry more weight. Dashed vertical markers show a change in the module set; compare across them with care. Toggle module lines in the legend to inspect their trends.

04 / METHODOLOGY

Read the trend, with context.

What each point measures

Each point is the mean of 90 Hyperfine executions after 30 warmups, on GitHub Actions Ubuntu runners with Python 3.14, in the checked-out repository. Neither benchmark measures a completed scaffold or dependency installation.

Runner variability and changes to the runner image or project checkout can affect comparisons. Sustained trends are more useful than an isolated increase.

What the comparisons mean

The summary compares the latest measurement with up to 100 preceding runs of the same benchmark. CI uses a separate check: 30 executions after 5 warmups, failing above 250% of the preceding recorded result. Historical tracking alerts above 200%.

Older wizard measurements remain in the JSON export, but are excluded from the recipe-editor chart and its comparisons.
